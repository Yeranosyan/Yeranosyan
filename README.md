## About

I am a Senior Software Engineer with more than six years of experience building
full-stack web applications. My work centers on replacing manual,
document-driven processes with interactive systems that customers and internal
teams rely on daily.

I develop modern, high-performing web applications and work closely with product
managers, designers, business stakeholders, and fellow engineers to deliver
seamless digital experiences. I take part in architectural discussions and
front-end design decisions, and help drive engineering best practices across the
organization.
